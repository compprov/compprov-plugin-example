## Audit Summary

This CPG implements a complete Ciddor/Birch-Downs refractive-index-of-air correction pipeline (Wexler/Sonntag saturation vapor pressure, Sellmeier dispersion for standard-air refractivity, CO2 correction, Birch-Downs pressure/thermal correction, CO2/water-vapor enhancement factor, Edlen water-vapor dispersion correction) feeding a two-color... actually single-wavelength (HeNe, 632.99 nm) interferometric length measurement with thermal-expansion correction to a gauge-block deviation-from-nominal result (`o_96`).

### Methodology
Per the task's focus on Topological & Provenance Fraud, I performed:
1. A full forward-consumption trace of every variable (`INPUT` and `OUTPUT`) against every operation's `arguments`/`resultId`, to detect M=0 (unconsumed mandatory contributors) and confirm the only true leaf is the declared terminal `o_96`.
2. A per-ID review of every variable flagged as consumed by >1 operation (`i_2, i_6, i_7, i_8, o_12, o_27, o_28, i_49`) to determine whether reuse constitutes genuine double-counting into the same terminal aggregation, or legitimate reuse of a physical quantity/constant across independent, non-competing formula branches.
3. A literal `resultId`-vs-`argument` check at every downstream node to rule out lineage disconnection/context substitution (i.e., confirming no operation consumes a hardcoded/root stand-in instead of the properly computed sibling with the same role).
4. A full manual numeric replay of the entire chain (T_K, T_K^2, Wexler polynomial, exp(), sigma/sigma^2, Sellmeier terms, N_s, CO2 correction, Birch-Downs P/T terms, N_tp, water-vapor enhancement factor, pv, Edlen N_v, N_total, n, lambda_air, half-wavelength, fringe order, L_raw, thermal factor, L_cal, deltaL) to confirm $O_{derived}$ matches every reported intermediate and the final $O_{reported}$ (`o_96 = 2.312175478646862285669288269`), ruling out silent substituted-context tampering that would still "locally replay" but diverge from true forward propagation.

### Findings
- **M = 0 (Omission):** No mandatory contributor is dropped. Every computed `OUTPUT` variable except the terminal `o_96` is consumed by exactly one downstream operation exactly once; every root `INPUT` is consumed at least once in its correct formula role. No dead-end leaves other than the intended terminal.
- **M > 1 (Double Counting):** All flagged multi-consumed variables (`i_2`='1' constant used in six independent "1+x" correction-factor formulas; `i_3`='2' constant; `i_6` lambda_vac used once for dispersion (via um-converted sigma) and once for direct lambda_air=lambda_vac/n conversion; `i_7`/`i_8` T_air/P_air reused across independent physical sub-formulas (Wexler, B&D pressure/thermal, enhancement factor); `o_12` T_K reused across the quadratic Wexler polynomial terms; `o_27`/`o_28` sigma/sigma^2 reused across Sellmeier dispersion and Edlen water-vapor terms; `i_49` 1e-8 scale factor reused in two distinct unit-scaling operations) are all legitimate reuse of a single physical quantity or genuine mathematical/unit constant across non-competing formula branches that converge exactly once into the terminal aggregation. None re-enter the same rollup twice.
- **Lineage Disconnection / Context Substitution:** The leaf-name-collision set is empty, and a broader role-based scan (T_air vs T_part vs T_ref; L_nom vs L_cal vs L_raw; xCO2 vs CO2 reference; multiple '1e-8'-type scale factors) found no computed sibling being bypassed in favor of a hardcoded root at a critical junction. Every downstream `arguments` entry literally references the correct upstream `resultId` (verified node-by-node, e.g., `op_55` consumes `o_87`/`o_93`, not a substituted constant; `op_56` consumes `o_94`/`i_95` as expected for the final deviation calculation).

No arithmetic manipulation or silent value swap detected.

### Non-structural observation (not a graph-topology finding)
The measured CO2 mole fraction (`i_10` = 450.0 ppm) exactly equals the correction-formula's reference concentration (`i_40` = 450 ppm), zeroing the CO2 correction term. This is a data-plausibility curiosity (worth a domain reviewer's attention) but is not itself evidence of M=0/M>1/context-substitution tampering — the correction pathway is present, wired correctly, and would apply nonzero correction for any other input value.
